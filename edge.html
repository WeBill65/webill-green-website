Why Webill Green

The Webill edge.

South Africa's electricity market is being built now. Webill Green is one of very few platforms that can carry a trade through it from end to end, and the only one we know of that does it on a single ledger, for every party.

Book a walkthrough The six advantages

Others handle a slice. Webill Green carries the whole trade.

A traded kilowatt-hour needs ten jobs done, in order, for every party, every month. Most tools do one or two of them; spreadsheets carry the trade between the rest.

Six advantages, each one built.

Every claim on this page points at something that exists in the product today, or says plainly when it is still to come.

One ledger for the whole book.

Bilateral PPAs, Eskom Virtual Wheeling, wholesale trades, network charges and certificates settle in one engine and land on one double-entry ledger. The month closes once, every party sees the same figures, and the Money Room builds its profit and loss from the same documents.

Ready for the wholesale market before it opens.

The Market Operator runs SAWEM's exchange. Webill Green is the participant's side of it, built to Market Code revision 2.3 and rehearsed every day against a simulated market of fifty participants. When the market opens, the desk is already built and rehearsed.

Neutral, so every party can use it.

Webill Green is not a generator, a trader or a wires owner, and it takes no position. A trader's platform serves its own book; Webill Green settles for the IPP, the trader and the buyer on the same terms, so both sides of a deal can sign the same statement.

Built for rules still being written.

Tariffs, loss factors, network charges, tax rates and draft trading rules live as effective-dated rows, not code. When NERSA finalises a rule or Treasury moves a rate, a row changes, and every past period still settles on the rules of its own dates.

Human sign-off and proof, built in.

NERSA's draft framework keeps automated trading off SAWEM for its first five years. Webill Green already works that way: engines compute, people approve, and four eyes sit on every limit. Every statement and pack carries a SHA-256 audit index and rebuilds byte for byte from its evidence.

Built in South Africa, made to travel.

The core is the same in every market: contracts, interval matching, a double-entry ledger, invoices and evidence. Each market changes the shell around it: the settlement grain, the operator, the certificates and the tax. Britain calls it a sleeved PPA. The engine does not care.
