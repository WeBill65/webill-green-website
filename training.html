Training and certification

Learn the platform on a live copy of it.

Thirty-eight self-paced modules, each taught in your own lab: a private, disposable copy of Webill Green seeded with one story year. You read a chapter, do the work on real screens, and the lab checks it. Certification then asks you to prove it on numbers no one else has.

Why training matters.

The platform runs the whole commercial stack of wheeling and trading. Its vocabulary is regulatory and its arithmetic ends in invoices. Reading the manual is not the same as being able to do the month-end.

  • Mistakes cost money.

    A wrong loss factor, a late bid or a certificate claimed twice is a real invoice, a market penalty or a false green claim. People should practise where a mistake costs nothing.

  • Customers need proof.

    A certificate shows a customer's auditors and internal controls that the person closing the month can do it correctly, without help.

  • The product moves every week.

    Every module records the release it was checked against, labs are re-checked each release, and short delta lessons keep certificates current.

How training works.

Self-paced and online, in as many sittings as you like. There is no classroom to wait for: everything an instructor would do lives in your lab.

One story, the Jacaranda year.

Every example, lab and exam question is a moment in one fictional year. A food company buys renewable energy from an IPP through a licensed trader, so the kilowatt-hours sent in one module are settled in another, billed in a third and certified in a fourth.

Jacaranda Foods
The buyer: a cold-storage distribution centre, a bakery with a coal boiler and 24 stores
Karoo Sun Power
The seller: solar, wind and a battery in the Northern Cape
Highveld Energy Traders
The trader in between, and the BRP that bids the market
Vaal Polymers
A second buyer, and the other side of every negotiation

All four are fictional. Eskom, municipalities, the market operators, NERSA and SARS appear in their real roles.

Your lab guide does an instructor's job.

  • Check my work reads your lab and marks each answer, with a hint for the usual mistake
  • Show the answer after two tries, worked from your own lab's numbers
  • Story events you trigger in order: the meter that stops, the late readings, the move to next month
  • Sign in as any seat with one click: the IPP, the trader or the buyer
  • Pause between sittings: the story's clock waits for you
  • Arithmetic by hand, then by machine: work the number in a spreadsheet, then find it on screen

Certification proves use, not attendance.

A certificate says that on this date, against this release, this person knew the concepts and did the work correctly without help. Finishing the course alone earns a certificate of completion, which claims nothing more.

  1. KnowledgeAn exam, pass mark 75 %20 to 30 scenario questions from the Jacaranda year: calculate the delivered kWh, read a screen, order the steps. Drawn at random from a bank three times the paper's size.
  2. UseA practical, pass mark 80 %Two to five tasks in a fresh lab whose numbers are unique to you, checked by the lab itself. Every critical task, such as the VAT on an invoice or a bid before gate closure, must pass.
  3. JudgementA written rationale150 to 300 words explaining a decision, marked against a rubric. The Master capstone adds a board presentation before two assessors.

  • Pass, Merit, Distinction.

    Merit needs 85 % in the exam and 90 % in the practical; Distinction 92 % and 100 % with an excellent rationale.

  • Valid for 24 months.

    Each certificate names the release it was earned on. Short delta lessons within 90 days keep it current as the product changes.

  • Verifiable by anyone.

    Issued as a PDF and an Open Badges 3.0 credential with a public verification link.

  • Fair by design.

    Every item is reviewed by two experts, piloted, and pass marks are set by the Angoff method. Appeals are heard within 14 days by a different assessor.

  • Hard to game.

    Identity checks for proctored assessments, randomised papers, labs unique to each candidate and an action log of how the work was done.

  • Your data stays here.

    Learner records are kept to the minimum POPIA allows and held in South Africa.
